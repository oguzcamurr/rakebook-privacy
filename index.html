Rakebook: Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026 · App: Rakebook: Poker Tracker (iOS) · Developer: Oguz Camur

Summary

Rakebook does not collect, sell or share any personal data, and stores nothing on a server. There is no account, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking.

Data on your device

Your sessions, results, bankrolls, deposits and withdrawals, saved hand ranges, Poker Academy progress, notes and settings are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. They are included in your device's iCloud or computer backups if you have those turned on; those backups are managed by Apple, not by Rakebook. The optional App Lock uses Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode through iOS; Rakebook never receives biometric data.

Usage statistics

This version of Rakebook does not send usage statistics or any other data. (More › Share Anonymous Usage Statistics controls whether a future version may send them.)

Crash and performance reports

iOS can deliver crash, hang and performance reports for Rakebook (Apple's MetricKit). They contain technical information such as stack traces, launch times and memory use, never your sessions or notes, and they stay on your device unless you choose to share one with support from More › Diagnostics. If you allow it in iOS settings, Apple may also share crash reports with the developer through App Store Connect.

Live Activities

If you use Rakebook Pro's Live Activity, your session timer and the amount in play are shown on your Lock Screen. The Live Activity is updated by the app on your device; no server is involved.

Exports and backups

If you use Back Up Now, Export (CSV) or Share a range, Rakebook creates a file or text and opens the system save or share sheet. Where it goes (Files, iCloud Drive, AirDrop, email) is your choice. Rakebook never uploads it anywhere.

Purchases

The optional Rakebook Pro purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store. Rakebook only checks with StoreKit whether Pro has been purchased on your Apple Account. It does not receive your payment details.

Children

Rakebook does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.

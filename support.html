Rakebook: Support

Questions, bug reports or ideas: oguzcamuur@outlook.com. Please include your iOS version and what you were doing.

Moving to a new phone

In Rakebook, open More › Back Up Now and save the file to iCloud Drive or Files. On the new phone, open More › Restore from Backup and choose that file. Rakebook checks the file before replacing anything and keeps a safety copy of the data it replaces. Backups are free for everyone.

How the numbers are calculated

Result = what you took out − what you put in. Net = result − dealer tips − expenses, the same way for cash games and tournaments. Hourly = net of timed sessions ÷ hours played (breaks excluded). Sessions without accurate times are left out of hourly rates. The full list is in the app under More › How the numbers are calculated.

Importing from a spreadsheet

Rakebook Pro imports CSV files whose first row names the columns (for example date, start, end, location, buy-in, cash-out, profit, tournament, payout, tips, expenses). Every row is shown in a preview before anything is saved. A template is in More › CSV format and template. Rakebook does not claim compatibility with other apps' export files.

Poker Tools

The equity calculator, pot odds, outs, range analyzer and preflop charts are for study, practice and reviewing hands after a session. Many card rooms do not allow devices or outside assistance during a hand; follow the rules of the game you play. The preflop charts are heuristic starting points, not solver (GTO) solutions.

Poker Academy

The Poker Academy (Tools › Poker Academy) is free and works offline: a 15-lesson beginner's guide, hand rankings with real cards, worked Hold'em examples, a searchable encyclopedia of poker terms and practice quizzes. Your lesson progress and best quiz scores are included in backups.

Reporting a problem

Email us what happened. If the app crashed or froze, More › Diagnostics lists technical reports iOS created for Rakebook; you can attach one to your email. They don't contain your sessions or notes.

Restoring Rakebook Pro

Pro is a one-time purchase tied to your Apple Account and supports Family Sharing. On a new device, open More › Restore Purchases.
